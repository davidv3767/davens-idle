# Davens' Idle - A Bouncy Ball Idle Game!

## What is Davens' Idle?
Created at Haven (haven.hackclub.com), this is an idle game where you gain bounces, upgrade your balls, & get the prestige levels! You can access it at https://davidv3767.github.io/davens-idle/.

## Stages

### 1. Reaching Bouncfinity
Through upgrading your balls (color from red --> black && size from S --> 3XL), you gain multipliers, allowing you to exponentially gain bounces. Once you get 2^37 bounces (about 137.44 billion), you can bouncfinate!

### 2. Early Bouncfinity
On the bouncfinity (1st prestige) tab, you can use your βP to increase bounce gain, bounce speed, or more βP gain. You may also do one of three challenges. The first challenge reduces your speed by half, the second limits your bouncy balls to a max of purple, 2XL ones, and the third steals 0.5% of your bounces every second! 

### 3. Late Bouncfinity
After all 3 challenges are beat, BOUNCFINITY IS BROKEN! You can gain more bouncfinities and more βP by waiting past 137.44 billion to bouncificate! This will fuel you towards the next sub-prestige level.

### 4. Daven Balls
Welcome the Daven bouncy balls! Inspired by the Haven mascot, there are 7 Daven ball types (normal, bronze, silver, gold, cosmic, shiny, royalty, & emperor) bought with βP that passively generate more βP for you!

### 5. Misc.
At the stats tab, you can check your achievements/secrets (37 achievements, 8 secrets) and the player index based off of them (from 0.0 to 5.1). You can also reset all your progress at any time by pressing "Reset Progress"! Please note there will be no warning; one press will reset everything.
